Core Safety Modules

Designed for student commuters, solo travelers, and everyday peace of mind.

Live Real-Time GPS

Continuous GPS location streaming via browser Geolocation API with accuracy circles, speed, heading, and breadcrumb trails.

Revocable Shared Links

Generate secure, time-expiring temporary links for trusted contacts. Revoke access instantly whenever you choose.

Smart Destination Geofencing

Enter any destination, compute optimal routes via OSRM, and receive automatic arrival notifications upon entering your destination zone.

Delay & Overdue Detection

Set an expected arrival time. If travel is delayed beyond your estimated window, safety notifications trigger for your circle.

Emergency SOS Dispatch

Deliberate 2-step confirmed SOS broadcast transmitting exact coordinates, timestamp, and map links directly to your emergency contacts.

Firebase Realtime Sync

Integrated with Firebase Realtime Database for instantaneous coordinate synchronization and Firebase Authentication.

User Privacy & Voluntary Tracking Guarantee

This system operates strictly with your explicit consent. Your location is never tracked in the background without permission. You can pause or stop location sharing at any time with a single tap. Location viewing links are private and can be revoked on demand.
